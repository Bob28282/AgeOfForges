How custom bonuses work
  • Targets and effects. A group (Cavalry, Archers, Buildings…), a unit line (Militia line, Knight line…) or one unit and the upgrades after it, one building, a villager job, techs — a single tech, an upgrade set, everything at one building, or the ages — or the whole civilization, for its population limit and one-time resources.
  • Several targets. Click more tiles to add them — Archers and Galleys, Knights and Varangian Guards. Units, buildings and villager jobs mix; techs mix only with techs. Only the effects every target can take are offered, and a unit two targets both cover (Cavalry and the Knight line) gets the bonus once.
  • Three places to use them. As a civ bonus, as a team bonus (step 4), or inside a Build Custom unique tech (steps 6–7), where it takes effect once researched. Tech, Civilization and Unique unit bonuses can't be team bonuses, and Civilization bonuses can't go in a unique tech.
  • When. A bonus can start at an age (Archers +15% HP from the Castle Age), grow each age (Militia line +5/+10/+15 HP in Feudal/Castle/Imperial — type the total at each age), or wait until a building is built (free Loom once a Mill is built). The building trigger fires once, when the first one is finished; losing it doesn't take the bonus back. These are built the way the game's own age bonuses are, and can't be team bonuses or go in a unique tech.
  • Card image. A card shows its targets' icons; Choose images swaps in one or two icons from the picker instead. It only changes the picture in Empire Forge — not what the bonus does or how it reads in game.
  • Groups match the game's own. "Cavalry" covers exactly what the game's cavalry bonuses do — including cavalry archers.
  • Civs keep a copy. Picking a bonus in the wizard copies it into the civ, so a civ file you share works for anyone. Editing or deleting a library bonus doesn't change civs that already use it; the wizard offers to update them.
  • Where they're saved: your Empire Forge data folder — one file holding your whole library. Clearing your browser doesn't touch it; back it up or move it to another computer like any file.
  • Sharing: the download button on a bonus exports just that one; Export all exports your whole library. Importing skips bonuses you already have.
